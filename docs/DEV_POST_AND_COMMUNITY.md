## 🎁 Hacktoberfest Stickers & Completionist++ FAQ

### 1. When do I get the physical sticker pack?
- **Timeline:** DEV and MLH review challenge submissions following the conclusion of Hacktoberfest (end of October / early November).
- **Claim Process:** Qualifying participants receive an email notification through DEV with a redemption code and shipping address collection link via the DEV Swag Store or MLH delivery partner.
- **Eligibility Criteria:** Publish valid submissions adhering to challenge rules and code-of-conduct guidelines during Hacktoberfest.

### 2. What is "Completionist++"?
- **Completionist++** is an elite community achievement badge awarded to contributors who successfully complete and publish submissions for **all 4 weekly DEV Hacktoberfest AI challenges**:
  - *Week 1:* "Touch Grass" (Arnav built **WildTrail**)
  - *Week 2:* "Side Hustle" (Arnav built **GhostLedger**)
  - *Week 3:* "Tools & Utility"
  - *Week 4:* "Wildcard / Showcase"
- Earning Completionist++ unlocks exclusive digital profile badges on dev.to and special physical swag items included in the Hacktoberfest distribution.
